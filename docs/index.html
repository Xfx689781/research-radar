About Research Radar. A free daily digest of new research for curious readers. Choose any of 80 topics across mathematics, statistics, computer science and AI, physics, astronomy, chemistry, biology, medicine, economics and engineering; every morning the newest papers in those topics appear here, each explained in plain words, and you can get them by email.

Papers come from arXiv, bioRxiv and medRxiv; AI news from the public feeds of AI labs and news sites, linked to the original articles. Summaries are written by AI from each abstract and can be wrong; open the paper before relying on them. Thank you to arXiv for use of its open access interoperability. Source on GitHub.
